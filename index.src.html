The key grips the lock at two points.

Why benzos and tranq change the plan

Narcan fits one lock. The supply hits three.

mu-opioid receptor, 7 helices
{{mol:lock_mu}}
Fentanyl, nitazenes, cychlorphine Stops the breathing rhythm. right lock: Narcan reverses
GABA-A receptor, a 5-subunit Cl⁻ pore
{{mol:lock_gaba}}
Xanax, bromazolam, ethylbromazolam A second brake: opens the chloride pore more often. wrong lock: Narcan does nothing
α2-adrenergic receptor, 7 helices
{{mol:lock_a2}}
Medetomidine, xylazine (tranq) A smaller pocket; same brake inside the cell as mu. wrong lock: Narcan does nothing

The plan for Black Rock City

Two kits per camp. One in four pockets. Say it.

2 + 1 two kits and a sober carrier on shift, per camp, stage and art car Two, because a kit can fail and strong opioids can need a second dose.
17,900 pockets carrying Narcan after dark: one in four Burners Covers every group of 16 or more, 99% of the time.
Say it a red patch on your bag; leaving camp at night, know who carries A kit nobody knows about doesn't count.
